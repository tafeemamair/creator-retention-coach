## Know which sentences cause viewers to scroll away
CRC scans every sentence to flag low-momentum phrasing, channel intros, filler bridges, and weak CTAs before you start filming.
Greetings waste the golden 0–3 second window where algorithmic swipe decisions are made.
Cut the intro entirely. Open with the tension: 'You are losing 30% of your gains on this one exercise.'
Meta-announcements tell viewers what you plan to do rather than delivering value.
Jump straight to the mechanism: 'Here is why your traditional bank account costs you money.'
Generic triple CTAs trigger ad fatigue and accelerate the final drop-off.
Use a single focused trigger: 'Save this reel so you remember to switch accounts this weekend.'
